Common Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

On Home, tap the gear button, then Proverbory Lifetime > Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account you bought it with.

Is there a subscription?

No. Proverbory Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for good. Family Sharing covers up to five family members.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is charged and nothing renews. Everything you saved stays. Adding past 25 cards, and the quiz modes beyond Quick Quiz and Daily Review, need Proverbory Lifetime.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone; Proverbory has no accounts. To keep a copy, go to Settings > Export (CSV or a full JSON backup). Import restores it.

Why didn't a new word get a written entry?

Entries for words outside the catalog are written on-device by Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Proverbory uses the Wiktionary definition instead.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, then choose Proverbory: Word of the Day, Saying of the Day, or Daily Mix.
